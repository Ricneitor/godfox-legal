GodFox Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 5, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how GodFox processes information when installed and used within communities on Root.

1. What GodFox is

GodFox is a community management, moderation, automation, organization, and engagement application designed for Root communities.

2. Information GodFox may process

Depending on which features are enabled by a community, GodFox may process information such as:

  • Root user identifiers and community identifiers.
  • Usernames, display names, avatars, roles, and membership information.
  • Moderation records such as warnings, kicks, bans, and internal case notes.
  • Reports, tickets, suggestions, polls, tasks, events, and reminders.
  • Configuration settings selected by community administrators.
  • Activity and audit logs generated by GodFox features.
  • Level, XP, profile, and participation data when those modules are enabled.

3. Why this information is used

GodFox processes information only to provide and operate its features, including moderation, logging, role management, automation, tickets, polls, suggestions, events, reminders, levels, and other community tools.

4. Moderation and audit data

GodFox may store moderation cases and audit records in order to help community staff review actions, maintain consistency, investigate reports, and keep a record of administrative activity.

5. Community-generated information

Content submitted through GodFox features, such as reports, ticket messages, suggestions, polls, tasks, and event details, may be stored when necessary for those features to function.

6. Data storage

GodFox stores application data using the storage and server environment provided for Root applications. Data is associated with the community in which GodFox is installed.

7. Authentication and credentials

GodFox does not request or store user passwords. Developer credentials, authentication tokens, API keys, and similar secrets are not intended to be stored as user-generated application data.

8. Data sharing

GodFox does not sell personal information. Information processed by GodFox is used to provide the application's functionality and may be visible to authorized community staff or other community members when a feature is designed to be public.

9. Data retention

Information may be retained for as long as it is necessary to provide the relevant feature, maintain moderation history, or satisfy the configuration chosen by the community administrators.

10. Data deletion

Community administrators may remove certain information through GodFox features where deletion controls are available. For additional data deletion requests, users may contact the GodFox developer.

11. Security

Reasonable technical measures are used to protect information processed by GodFox. Administrative actions and sensitive operations are designed to be validated by the server and restricted according to permissions.

12. Children's privacy

GodFox is not designed to knowingly collect information from children outside of the normal information required to operate within a Root community. Community owners are responsible for ensuring that their use of GodFox complies with applicable rules, laws, and platform policies.

13. Third-party platform

GodFox operates within Root and depends on Root's infrastructure and APIs. Root may independently process information according to its own policies and terms.

14. Changes to this policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when GodFox adds new functionality, changes how data is processed, or needs to reflect new technical or legal requirements.
